A reproducible research process

Order-book prediction,
with an evidence trail.

Can orders waiting to buy and sell help predict whether the mid-price moves down, stays within a no-change band or moves up, 10 observed book states later? This offline report compares simple methods and records how the evidence was checked.

An order book lists waiting buy and sell orders; a book state is one snapshot of that list. Mid-price means halfway between the best buy and sell prices. “Unchanged” includes small moves inside the frozen target threshold, not only identical prices. Exact threshold and tick-size settings appear in the configuration record. This report places no orders.

What the data covers

This record contains 12 observed dates and 7 development evaluation dates. The intended real-data study uses noncontiguous opening excerpts from month-start files, not complete trading days or a continuous backtest. Many correlated updates do not create many independent dates.

Comparison / same eligible samples

How did the methods compare?

Both scores range from 0 to 1; higher is better. Macro F1 balances false alarms and missed outcomes, giving down, unchanged and up equal weight. Balanced accuracy averages the fraction correctly recognized in each actual class present in that evaluation period. Missing classes are disclosed in warnings.

The majority baseline always predicts the most common training outcome. Logistic regression is the simple linear comparison. Small boosted trees model more flexible relationships. A score improvement is not a return or profit estimate.

Warnings and qualifications

  • Only noncontiguous monthly opening excerpts are observed; these are not full trading days.
  • Millions of correlated rows are not millions of independent experimental observations.
  • Date bootstrap intervals are exploratory; overlapping training windows and few dates limit inference.
  • Vendor snapshots were reconstructed and may have crossed levels removed upstream.
  • CSV disconnect events are unavailable; a 30-second gap rule is only a discontinuity heuristic.
  • Order sizes are vendor-native USD notional for this inverse instrument, not BTC quantities.
  • Prediction scores do not establish executable, fee-adjusted trading profitability.
  • No fitted models, row-level predictions or historical records are redistributed.
  • Exploratory session/date bootstrap: assumes exchangeable independent evaluation units; overlapping training folds do not create independent observations.
  • Fewer than 10 session/date units; the percentile interval may be unstable and must not be read as proof of generalization.

Research note / evidence before claims

Inconclusive evidence of improvement beyond the linear comparison

The frozen comparisons do not jointly support a repeatable improvement beyond logistic regression. Any observed advantage is limited by variation across dates, a small independent sample, or missing-class coverage.

Next test

Predeclare a new set of broader intraday dates and a new untouched holdout before further model selection.

Research findings are conditional on the observed instrument, dates, excerpt selection, target definition and data transformations. Classification scores alone establish neither executable latency nor fee-adjusted trading profitability.
